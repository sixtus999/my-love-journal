Questions

Where is my diary stored?

In this browser, on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Use Backup in Settings to download a copy, because clearing your browser data deletes the diary.

How do we both write in it?

Each of you writes in your own copy and picks your name on each page. Export your diary, send the file to your partner, and they import it. Pages merge by their unique ID, so nothing is duplicated. Live editing at the same time would need accounts and a server, which this version does not have.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser. Each device keeps its own copy until you export and import.

Are the photo looks AI-generated?

No. They are color filters applied to your photo when it is shown, and your original photo is kept.
